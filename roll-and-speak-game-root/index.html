📖 Game Rules

  • Goal: Be the first team/player to navigate through space 20 at the end of the forest path!
  • Rolling: Click "Roll the Die!" or press Spacebar on your turn to roll 1–6.
  • Speaking Questions (💬): Answer the prompt out loud in English before your turn ends.
  • Special Forest Events:
    • 🍄 Mushroom: Move backward / forward or act out.
    • 🌰 Acorn: Move forward or back space.
    • 🍂 Swirling Leaf: Take a bonus roll!
    • 🦔 Sleepy Hedgehog: Take a rest (skip next turn).
    • 🦉 Wise Owl: Ask another player a question!
    • 🐿️ Squirrel Sprint: Bonus forward move!
